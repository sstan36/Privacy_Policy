Summary

The App is built around BaZi (Four Pillars) chart generation. Other features, such as narrative readings, may not be available in your build; only what we actually ship and describe in the App Store and this Policy applies to you.

We do not sell your personal information and do not use it for cross-app or behavioural advertising through data brokers. We process data to run the App (charts, optional purchases, reliability, and support you initiate). We do not ask for access to your contacts, photo library, or precise location for chart features.

If you use Contact support in Settings, you may choose to send a short on-device diagnostic excerpt and basic device or app metadata. We design that log to avoid chart payloads, birth details, and similar sensitive content. See the section on support below.

1. Who we are

This Policy applies to Michael Tan and the TianJi iOS application, including use of Google Firebase and, where relevant, Apple services (App Store, StoreKit). Contact details are in Section 16 at the end of this page.

2. Scope

The App lets you create and manage BaZi charts with traditional computational methods and optional online support. This Policy covers:

  • Information processed on your device;
  • Information sent to Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Functions, Firestore, and related Google Cloud infrastructure as configured for our project); and
  • Information you voluntarily email us when you contact support.

It does not govern third-party websites or services we do not control.

3. Categories of information

We describe data in plain language consistent with how Apple and many laws refer to personal information or personal data. Information you enter to generate a chart (such as birth date, birth time, timezone, and gender) can identify or relate to a person. We use it only as described here, not for unrelated marketing profiles or resale.

3.1 What you provide (chart inputs)

When you create or edit a chart, you may enter an optional label or name; date of birth, time of birth, timezone, and gender (needed for calculation); and notes stored locally with the chart. Avoid entering more sensitive data than you need; you are responsible for accuracy.

3.2 On your device

We use Apple frameworks to store data on your device, including Core Data (charts and related metadata), Keychain (identifiers related to entitlements, such as chart slots or installation correlation), and UserDefaults or similar (preferences such as disclaimer acceptance and language). Unless sent to our servers or exported by you, we cannot access purely on-device content.

3.3 On our servers (Firebase)

When you use backend features (for example generating or validating a chart, or syncing purchase-related entitlements), processing may include: a Firebase Anonymous Authentication identifier; app version and a device installation correlation identifier for support and consistent entitlements after reinstall or restore; Firestore fields needed to run the product (such as counts or limits, sync timestamps, and server-maintained integrity fields); and request payloads needed for the operation (typically birth-related inputs and structured BaZi data from computation). Chart inputs and outputs are sensitive; we use encryption in transit and reasonable access controls on the backend.

3.4 Technical data

Like most networked apps, Google (Firebase / Google Cloud) and we may process technical data such as IP address, request metadata, and, depending on your build configuration, diagnostics or stability data if those products are linked. See Firebase Privacy and Security. We do not intend to use such data for advertising profiling at launch; material changes will be reflected here and, where required, in the App.

4. Support diagnostics and email

You may use Settings → Contact support (if available) to email us. Nothing is sent automatically; you decide whether to send the message. The composer may attach device and app metadata (for example model, iOS version, app version, locale) and a recent excerpt of an on-device diagnostic log. We aim not to place raw chart JSON, birth dates or times, prompt text, or similar identifying chart content in that log; entries should be short technical summaries to help reproduce issues.

If you type personal information in the email body, we use it only to respond and for ordinary support and legal retention, subject to applicable law.

5. How we use information

We use information to generate, validate (if enabled), display, and store BaZi charts; maintain anonymous authentication, rate limits, entitlements, and ledger consistency where those exist; operate, secure, and improve the service; respond to support requests; and comply with law and our Terms of Use. Chart output is not medical, legal, financial, or other professional advice; see the Terms of Use and Disclaimer in the App.

6. Generative AI and readings

At launch this Policy describes chart-focused processing. We do not separately describe AI narrative reading products here. If we enable readings or other generative-AI features in a public build, we will update this Policy and, where required, App Store disclosures and in-app notice before or when that happens.
